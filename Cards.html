++ Systems & games

C++

High-performance software with fine control over memory, hardware, and speed.

  • Compiled and highly performant
  • Popular for engines and embedded systems
  • Supports object-oriented and generic styles

Choose C++ when low-level control and maximum performance are central to the project.

# Apps & services

C#

A modern, expressive language for polished applications, games, and connected services.

  • Runs on the cross-platform .NET runtime
  • Strong tooling and automatic memory management
  • Used with Unity, web, desktop, and cloud apps

Choose C# when you want a productive language with a broad ecosystem and excellent developer tools.
